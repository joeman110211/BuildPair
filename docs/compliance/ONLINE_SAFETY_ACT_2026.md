# BuildPair Online Safety Act compliance record

Status: current working assessment  
Assessment date: 4 October 2026  
Accountable owner: BuildPair founder/director  
Next scheduled review: 4 October 2027, or earlier if a review trigger below occurs

> This record documents BuildPair's current risk assessment and proportionate safety measures. It is an operational compliance record, not a substitute for specialist legal advice.

## 1. Current service state

BuildPair is a UK home-improvement marketplace and business platform. Tradespeople can currently sign up and join the launch/waiting-list experience. Homeowner job posting is implemented but is not yet generally open while launch approvals and regulatory dependencies are completed.

The product includes or is designed to include:
- public tradesperson profiles and portfolio media;
- job descriptions and job photos;
- project-linked reviews;
- private job-scoped messaging between marketplace participants;
- quotes, invoices, project records and payment-related workflows;
- reporting, moderation, warnings, messaging restrictions and account suspension.

Because BuildPair allows user-generated information to be shared with other users, this assessment conservatively treats the relevant marketplace features as an in-scope user-to-user service for Online Safety Act compliance planning.

## 2. Governance and review triggers

The BuildPair founder/director is accountable for online-safety governance while the service remains a small operator.

This assessment must be reviewed:
- at least annually;
- before a significant change to messaging, public posting, media upload, recommender/discovery systems or user access;
- before launching a feature likely to materially change child access or harmful-content exposure;
- after a serious safety incident or material moderation failure;
- when Ofcom materially updates its risk profiles or applicable Codes of Practice.

Changes, incidents, moderation outcomes and resulting safety measures should be recorded in the repository or the appropriate operational record.

### Current proportionate illegal-content measures

- authenticated account controls for marketplace participation;
- job-scoped participant checks around private messaging;
- automated message-safety detection for explicit threats, scam pressure, intimidation and targeted abuse;
- automated image moderation before accepted job/profile images are stored;
- blocking of explicit sexual/nude content, graphic violence, hate/extremist imagery, threatening or weapon-focused imagery, illegal-drug-focused imagery and sensitive personal information;
- removal of EXIF/GPS/XMP metadata from uploaded images before storage;
- upload and write rate limits;
- public no-account safety reporting through the Report page;
- authenticated account-linked reporting for marketplace incidents;
- an admin moderation queue with warnings, messaging restrictions, conversation closure and account suspension/restoration;
- Terms of Use and Marketplace Standards that prohibit illegal, abusive, fraudulent, misleading and unsafe content and explain enforcement.

## 4. Children's access assessment

BuildPair's marketplace and account tools are intended for adults aged 18 or over. BuildPair does not currently use highly effective age assurance across the public service.

For Online Safety Act purposes, BuildPair therefore does **not** rely on its 18+ contractual rule as proof that children cannot access the service. The conservative assessment is that at least the public website and public user-generated marketplace material may be accessed by children.

Outcome: **likely to be accessed by children** for compliance planning.

That means BuildPair should continue to apply the protection-of-children duties proportionately rather than treating the service as child-excluded merely because its Terms say 18+.

## 5. Children's risk assessment

BuildPair is not designed to attract children, does not provide a general social feed, does not recommend entertainment content, and its core purpose is arranging home-improvement work. The main plausible exposure paths are public profile/job media, public text and private messaging if an under-18 person were to obtain access.

### Child-safety design position

- Account and marketplace use is contractually restricted to users aged 18+.
- That rule is not represented as highly effective age assurance.
- Public content remains subject to safety moderation and reporting.
- BuildPair should reassess age assurance before adding features that materially increase child appeal, anonymous interaction, open discovery or high-risk content exposure.

## 6. CSEA detection and NCA reporting procedure

From 7 April 2026, in-scope user-to-user providers have a legal reporting duty for qualifying child sexual exploitation and abuse content/activity detected on their services.

If BuildPair detects or becomes aware of suspected CSEA material or grooming activity:

1. Do not ask staff, users or reporters to download, forward or reproduce suspected CSAM.
2. Restrict access to the relevant content/account where technically and legally appropriate.
3. Preserve only the records necessary for safety, investigation, legal compliance and reporting.
4. Escalate immediately to the accountable safety owner.
5. Check whether the material/activity has already been reported through the applicable statutory route and avoid unnecessary duplicate handling where the reporting rules require this.
6. Submit a report through the NCA Child Sexual Exploitation & Abuse Industry Reporting Portal (CSEA-IRP) where the statutory reporting duty applies.
7. Where a child or other person appears to face immediate danger, contact the emergency services / police rather than relying on the platform reporting process.
8. Record the incident, action taken, reporting reference and any follow-up safety change without storing unnecessary illegal material in the compliance record.

**External action still required:** register the BuildPair organisation and an authorised administrator for the NCA CSEA-IRP. Registration is an external NCA account process and cannot be completed by a repository change.

## 7. Reporting and complaints

BuildPair provides:
- a public Report page allowing a person to report illegal or harmful content without an account by emailing the safety/support address;
- an authenticated marketplace-report form that can be linked to a BuildPair user and relevant platform context;
- a moderation complaints route through info@buildpair.co.uk for review of a safety-report outcome or moderation decision;
- emergency wording telling users to call 999 where someone is in immediate danger.

Safety reports should be triaged according to seriousness, with illegal-content and immediate-risk reports prioritised over ordinary marketplace disputes.

## 9. Significant-change review (proposed regional marketplace opening, 8 October 2026)

**Status: CONDITIONAL, not approved for public activation.** This is an additional pre-change risk review of enabling actual homeowner job posting, quote exchange, private job-scoped messaging and direct-payment job records across Surrey, London and neighbouring regions. BuildPay remains unavailable.

**Exposure changes:** Public job descriptions and media increase risks of illegal posts, personal data exposure and content viewable by children. Private messaging and sharing addresses can facilitate harassment, stalking, fraud and abuse. Scams and user-to-user contact carry at least medium inherent risk pending incident evidence; address disclosure can have high-impact consequences.

**Children:** BuildPair serves adults but its public marketplace cannot be assumed inaccessible to children without effective age assurance. Existing child-access and children's-risk findings continue to apply; monitor public user-generated content closely.

**Safeguards to validate:** Private address redaction, role/participant authorization on messages and jobs, media screening and metadata stripping, anti-spam limits, abuse reporting without requiring an account, admin moderation escalation, account restrictions and incident logs. Users arranging direct payments must be told that BuildPair does not hold, guarantee, refund or protect those funds.

**Mandatory pre-opening checks:** Confirm in-scope NCA CSEA-IRP organisation and administrator registration and response procedure; ICO assessment/fee status; test safety and abuse controls with real non-admin QA accounts; appoint a person responsible for daily moderation; verify independent BuildPay endpoint shutdown; log founder sign-off after final tests.

**Assessment decision:** Existing controls plausibly support a small controlled release after those checks, but implementation and external registrations are not independently verified. Do not open the marketplace or make unqualified safety promises until all required checks are complete. Reassess after a serious incident or a material new feature.
